About Curious Minds

Curious Minds writes books for kids ages 7 to 12 and the grown-ups who choose them. Our story collections follow ordinary children through problems they will recognise: a voice that shakes when reading aloud, being left out at recess, a first try that does not work. Each one tries the easy thing first, it falls short, and then they find something that does.

Our true-story books introduce young readers to real people who asked questions, built things, and kept going when it got hard.

  • The child solves it. No adult arrives with the right speech at the end.
  • The first attempt fails. That is the part worth reading.
  • Problems can start on a screen. They get fixed in a room — out loud, with a person.
  • One age per book. A seven-year-old and a ten-year-old do not need the same story.
